# HF Search 🔍

**Hugging Face search, but make it not terrible.**

![HF Search in action](screenshot.png)

---

Ever searched Hugging Face for something and gotten *1,800 results* — 1,768 of which are junk from the same couple of spammy accounts? Yeah. The official search gives you no way to make them go away. You just suffer.

**HF Search fixes that.**

## The magic trick ✨

Search **"krea"** → 1,800 results.
Mute 2 spammy uploaders → **1,768 of them vanish.**
You're left with the **32 that actually matter.**

That's the whole pitch.

## What you get

- 🔇 **Mute annoying uploaders** — one click and every result from them is gone (until you change your mind).
- 🚫 **Hide junk words** — never want to see `gguf` or `merge` again? Banished.
- 🖼️ **Actually see the models** — real preview thumbnails for image stuff. Hover for a bigger look.
- 🔀 **Two looks** — a tidy list or a big-thumbnail gallery. Flip with one button.
- 🌗 **Light, dark, or auto** — follows your computer like a polite app should.
- 🔎 **Real filters** — sort by trending, most downloaded, most liked, or newest; filter by type and tags.
- 🔑 **Optional token** — paste a Hugging Face token for higher limits. Totally optional, no pressure.

## How to use it

Open **`index.html`** in your browser. Search. Mute the riff-raff. Done.

No install, no sign-up, no server, nothing to run. It's **one file** — you could text it to a friend right now.
